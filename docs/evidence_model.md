# Evidence Model

## Evidence strength levels
0 not found; 1 file/path indicator; 2 code indicator; 3 test indicator; 4 negative/security test; 5 demo attack; 6 CI evidence; 7 staging/deployment evidence; 8 telemetry/audit evidence; 9 external validation.

## Evidence sources
Files, tests, security tests, demo attacks, CI logs, deployment records, audit/telemetry records, and external reports.

## Weak evidence
Path names, filenames, and isolated keywords are weak evidence and do not prove a control works.

## Strong evidence
Strong evidence combines implementation, tests, negative tests, demo attacks, CI, runtime telemetry, and review.

## What cannot be claimed without tests
Control effectiveness and regression resistance cannot be claimed without tests.

## What cannot be claimed without CI
Portfolio-proof repeatability cannot be claimed without CI evidence.

## What cannot be claimed without staging
Runtime readiness, rollback, monitoring, and operational behavior cannot be claimed without staging evidence.

## What cannot be claimed without external validation
Enterprise production-candidate readiness, certification, and compliance cannot be claimed without qualified external validation.
